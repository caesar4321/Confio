## Problem Statement

Store in-app purchases are gone (client 69fb14ac, server 1eb05f7c): Duende Limited
(Seychelles) can't sell through Google Play, and AI sold as an app feature falls under
store billing. The founder wants Confío's own recurring revenue and believes Confío IA
("Personal Finance Muse") is what people would pay for. The proposed vehicle is
"Cuenta inteligente" (working name at session start: Cuenta Confío), a ~US$10/month membership billed from the user's own wallet through a
user-authorized monthly debit, whose store-safe SKU is financial and whose visible
value is Confío IA (the Nubank+ pattern: a paid financial membership with an AI perk).

The open question is not how to bill it. It is whether anyone will pay for it at all.

## Demand Evidence

Prod, measured 2026-10-07. Founder (user 5) excluded everywhere.

- **Willingness to pay monthly: none observed.** No user has asked to pay a monthly fee
  or for recurring payments.
- **One-time payments do happen.** Since 2026-09-15, 6 real users paid the one-time
  10 cUSD local-account activation (Brazil 5 activations, Colombia 4, one Mexico by the
  founder). 4 of the 6 paid on the day they joined; one active Brazil user paid twice
  (BR, then CO). 0 opened accounts were left unpaid.
- **Confío IA, first 3 days (since 2026-10-04):** 23 turns from 11 real users.
  - 7 support or how-to ("cargué saldo desde mi Binance y no llega", "cómo funciona la
    aplicación", verification stuck months).
  - 1 loan request.
  - 2 investing: user 4 (Argentina, joined 2025-09) had 8 turns in 15 minutes on stocks,
    "¿cuál acción me recomendarías?", gold, QQQ and diversification; user 11942 asked
    what a stock is.
  - 1 savings pain, user 11947 (Peru, joined that day): "Tengo dinero guardado en casa.
    Para tenerlo en esta app debo depositarlo en mi banco. Pero mi banco me cobrará una
    comisión muy fuerte para convertir mis soles en dólares."
- **Need tagger (30 days of support):** loans are the #1 unmet need (24 people, all never
  funded); "how does it work" is #2.
- **Who pays Confío today (90 days of conversion fees, 0.9% in/out):** 58 users paid
  $736. **The top 6 paid 93%.** The top user paid $235 on $26,110 of volume (~$78 a
  month); the top three average $43–78 a month each. Everyone else paid ~$50 combined.
  Confío also keeps 15% of Confío Dollar+ yield.

## Status Quo

Funded users mostly park dollars (Confío Dollar+). Their recurring payments (rent,
family, bills) live in their bank, Yape or Nequi, not in Confío. Heavy movers pay
per-transaction fees today. Mass users rarely fund at all (~54 ever topped up; ~1.75
first fundings a week).

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- **Who to learn from:** the people already showing intent: the 2 investing IA users,
  the Peru saver, the 6 local-account payers, and the 6 heavy movers.
- **Narrowest wedge:** no product yet. A shown offer — "Cuenta inteligente, US$9.99/mes" —
  at the two places intent can show up, with an "Avísame" tap. No billing code.

## Constraints

- No store billing: anything sold for AI must not look like an app feature unlock
  (Apple 3.1.1, Google Play Billing). A future paid SKU must be financial in substance.
- Apple 2.1 (app completeness) can reject placeholder "coming soon" features. The door
  is a working waitlist (it records interest), is shown to App Review and declared in
  the review notes. If review rejects it anyway, the server flag turns it off for the
  resubmission and the test runs on Android only.
- A membership must not discount the heavy movers (premise 1).
- Ships in the next app build; visibility controlled from the server.

## Premises

1. Confío already earns recurring revenue: conversion fees (6 heavy movers = 93%) and
   the 15% Confío Dollar+ yield share. Any membership must not discount the 6 who pay.
2. For the mass base nothing paid is proven; local accounts become a free acquisition
   hook once Mesa de pagos removes the opening and maintenance cost (founder: a monthly
   fee "just to send money... outnumbers the large majority of competitors").
3. Confío IA is engagement today, not yet a paid product.
4. The next deliverable is evidence, not billing code.

## Cross-Model Perspective

Cold read by a fresh Claude subagent (Codex unavailable until 2026-10-11):

- **Steelman:** Confío wants to be the main dollar account for Latin Americans whose
  income and savings sit in weak currencies; recurring revenue comes from the flow, and
  a membership packages that flow for those who move the most.
- **Most revealing fact:** "the top 6 users paid 93%" of fees.
- **Challenged premise #2:** 6 of 6 paid the one-time activation with no drop-off, so the
  fee looks like pricing power and a filter for intent; it proposed testing 20–25 cUSD.
  The founder kept premise #2 and redirected the session: the point is Confío IA, not
  local-account pricing.
- **Its 48-hour plan,** adopted in part: ask heavy movers to pay a first month by hand;
  a fake door shown to funded users; skip recurring-debit code, AI paywalls and loans.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: Fake door inside Confío IA** — chosen, extended (see below).
- **B: Build Cuenta inteligente now** (membership + monthly user-authorized debit) — deferred
  until the fake door shows demand at a known price; ~4–6 human weeks for a money-moving
  surface nobody has asked for yet.
- **C: IA sells, isn't sold** (no IA price; measure IA → funding / Confío Dollar+ /
  stock conversions) — kept as the fallback if the fake door is weak.

## Recommended Approach

**Two products, two in-app probes, one event log** (founder, 2026-10-07, after the
benefits review and the Claude/ChatGPT exchange): the store question must not decide
the product, so the test first asks which paid job people want, then billing is solved
head-on for the winner.

- **Confío IA+**: a more capable personal financial assistant (what Assistant+ is on the
  server). Sold openly as AI; nothing about it is hidden or reworded.
- **Cuenta inteligente**: automatic payments and debits (the financial product).

Both are pure intent probes like the local-rail "Próximamente" rows: no money moves, no
balance, no contract, no billing code before launch (eng review D7). Every eligible
user can see both, each in its natural place (founder): Confío IA+ inside the assistant,
Cuenta inteligente in Billeteras. The founder accepts the App Review risk of showing a
priced AI subscription in the app (see Store safety).

### Store safety
- Not hidden from App Review. Review notes: "Confío IA+ and Cuenta inteligente are
  upcoming offers. These screens only record interest (a waitlist). Nothing is sold,
  charged or unlocked in the app." Same behavior on iOS and Android.
- **Accepted risk (founder):** a priced AI subscription shown in-app can be read as a
  call to action for a digital purchase outside IAP (3.1.1), even as a waitlist. Each
  probe has its own server flag (`IA_PLUS_PROBE_ENABLED`, `SMART_ACCOUNT_PROBE_ENABLED`),
  so if review objects, Confío IA+ is turned off alone for the resubmission and the
  Cuenta inteligente probe ships.
- Chips never appear at a usage limit and never unlock anything.

### For Build B (launch only; nothing below is built for the probe)
Decisions made during the reviews that apply when the real account is built:
- **If Confío IA+ wins, its billing is solved head-on, not routed through Cuenta
  inteligente:** in Brazil, Apple's CADE settlement (iOS 26.5, June 2026) allows
  third-party payments or link-outs for digital goods (~15% / 10% commission) and
  Google Play's alternative billing covers Brazil; elsewhere Apple IAP (if Duende can
  use it) or a new entity. First verify whether an in-wallet debit qualifies as an
  allowed alternative payment. It is never sold as an unlock paid from the wallet
  outside those programs (3.1.1). `AssistantSubscription` + `assistant/billing.py`
  already hold the entitlement.
- **If Cuenta inteligente wins:** its paid copy sells no AI usage; Confío IA appears
  only as how the account works (watching the user's payments and debits).
- The account parks **Confío Dollar+ (cUSD+)** so it keeps earning; only at payment time
  does it move cUSD+ → cUSD (→ USDT when the payee's rail needs it) (decision 8, revised).
  Users not eligible for Confío Dollar+ hold Confío Dollar (cUSD) (eng review D3).
- Moves between the user's own cUSD and cUSD+ are not conversions, so no 0.9% fee
  (issue 18; `CusdPlusVault.sol:520-521`).
- The monthly charge reuses the activation-fee path (`payment_accounts/activation.py`):
  a plain cUSD transfer to a fee-only collector like `AccountActivationCollector`; if
  cUSD is short, the whole charge is redeemed from cUSD+ (`activation_unwrap_plan`).
- The user-owned vault follows `ConfioPayrollVault`'s per-owner escrow pattern, with its
  freeze re-check (audit P2, lines 217-224) and a return path that can pay only the owner
  (as `ConfioInviteEscrow.reclaimInvitation`).
- If the set-aside idea returns, spends are never blocked by it (eng review D4).

- **The fair comparison is among funded users who were shown both offers** (placements

## Success Criteria

Two signals, decided in order. Pre-registered before the build.

**1. The Assignment (this week, before any code):** the ~9 people see both offers and
are asked which they would pay US$9.99/mes for at launch.

| Commitments to one offer (of ~9 asked) | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥3 for the same offer | Build that offer (IA+: solve billing; Cuenta: Build B); probes skipped |
| 1–2 for either | Build both in-app probes; decide after them |
| 0 for both | Drop both memberships; pursue C (IA sells, isn't sold); skip the probes |

**2. The in-app probes (4 weeks from the build reaching ≥60% of weekly active users):**
the primary signal per offer is funded users who answered "Sí" to "¿te suscribirías hoy
por US$9.99/mes?", confirmed in the founder's follow-up.

| Funded would-pay "Sí", confirmed (per offer) | Decision |
|---|---|
| ≥3 for one offer | Build that offer; if both reach ≥3, build the one with more |
| 1–2 for the leading offer | One more month with the same copy, once; then ≥3 → build, else drop |
| 0 for both | Drop both memberships; pursue C |

Also reported, never deciding alone: "Sí, avísame" ÷ unique users shown, per offer and
door, funded vs unfunded, by country.

### R2-1 — clarity (blocking)

**Problem**

> The fake-door decision table still has gaps and overlaps: a rate of >=10% with exactly 2 follow-up 'I'd pay' has no row (the >=10% row needs >=3 people, the 3-10% row excludes >=10%); exactly 10% falls in both '>=10%' and '3-10%'; and 'Run one more month ... then decide' does not say which rule decides after the extra month or whether a second extension is allowed. The original R1-5 obligation (a complete table covering mixed results) is still unmet.

**Remedy**

> Make the bands mutually exclusive and exhaustive (e.g. >=10%, 3-<10%, <3%) with a row for every follow-up count, and state that the extra month is decided by the same table with no further extension (or what happens instead).

### R2-2 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> The new Store safety decision shows the door to App Review, but the unchanged Constraints still say Apple 2.1 can reject visible 'coming soon' placeholders and that the door 'must be server-flagged and hidden from the App Review demo account'. The document now commits to two opposite behaviors, and the 2.1 rejection risk that motivated hiding is no longer addressed.

**Remedy**

> Update the Constraints to match the chosen behavior and state why a declared waitlist screen is acceptable under 2.1 (or what the fallback is if review rejects it, e.g. turning the flag off for the resubmission).

### R2-3 — consistency (blocking)

**Problem**

> The Assignment terms promise payers an automatic full refund, but the mechanism described is a manual list the founder 'refund[s] from ... if needed'; nothing in Confío performs an automatic refund. The terms also do not say where the 'normal send' goes (which wallet/account receives it), and they only refund at 2026-12-31 even if the membership is dropped earlier (Assignment 0 payers is impossible for payers, but the fake-door path can drop B in November), so payers' money can sit for weeks after a drop decision.

**Remedy**

> State the receiving wallet, replace 'automatically' with the actual commitment (founder refunds by a named date), and add that payers are refunded within N days of any drop decision, not only at 2026-12-31.

### R2-4 — clarity (blocking)

**Problem**
