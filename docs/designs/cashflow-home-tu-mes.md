## Problem Statement

Takenos and Meru move money and explain nothing. LATAM PFM apps (Kane, ualet,
Organizze, Sumak, Finly) explain money and cannot move it; Kane and ualet state
outright that they are read-only. Confío already owns the ledger: every deposit,
send, QR payment and withdrawal happens on its own rails, so the data is
structured at birth. That needs no bank scraping, no Open Finance and no SMS parsing.

Phase 1 turns that advantage into something a user sees: a month view of money
in and out, plus a protection number only Confío can compute. It ships timed to
the Bolivia QR and Venezuela rail launches.

Long arc: **Own it → Understand it → Prove it.** The closest analog is Toss's
second act, not Mint: transfers first, then understanding, then the credit-score
style proof. This doc covers *Understand it* only.

## Demand Evidence (prod, read-only aggregates, pulled 2026-10-03)

- MAU by country (Oct 2): **BO 214, VE 162**, PE 56, AR 38, CO 36, BR 28, MX 27.

  the Koywe on-ramp works (10 completed orders in 90d) but QR payments (Stereum) are pending; Venezuela's rails are pending. September
  signups were led by BO (192) and VE (144).
- Last 90 days: only 57 users touched a confirmed send and there were 26
  user→user sends, because the markets that hold the users can't move money yet.
- Of the 65 distinct sender→recipient pairs from user-originated confirmed sends, the
  24 pairs with 3+ payments carry **81% of those sends** (46% of the dollars). Caveat:
  recipient type `external` includes withdrawals to the user's own outside wallets, so this
  overstates person-to-person repetition. It is directional evidence, not proof.
- Human-written memos: ~0. All 230 memos are system text ("Depósito USDT recibido").
  Nobody labels payments voluntarily today.
- Koywe on-ramp 228 completed / 638 rejected over 41 / 37 users. Rejections are

Honest gap: this proves people are waiting, not that they want a month view.
Phase 1 has to measure that (see Success Criteria).

## Status Quo

A Bolivian or Venezuelan user today checks a balance and a transaction list. Nothing
answers "¿me alcanza hasta la quincena?" or "¿cuánto me protegí al pasar a dólares?".
Competitors in their market show the same balance-plus-list.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

The Bolivia QR payer in their first month after launch: many small payments,
mostly to people (comedores, transport, stalls), holding dollars because of
Bolivia's dollar shortage. Wedge: one hero line, a "Tu mes" screen, and the
protection value. No AI, no GPS, no clustering.

## Premises (agreed)

1. Activation is gated by rails, not UI. The redesign rides the Bolivia QR and Venezuela launches.
2. Design for Bolivia QR payers first: dense, small-ticket, person-to-person.
3. Home keeps Enviar/Recibir and the tabs (09-23 IA). Home changes by **one neutral line in the hero**.
4. Classification comes from known rails plus remembered counterparty answers. Undetermined
   **outbound** payments get asked inline (Decision 4). Inbound is labeled only by manual edit in phase 1.
5. GPS/Places and Constancia/reputation are out of phase 1.

## Approaches Considered

- **A: "Tu mes" screen only, Home untouched.** Rejected: hidden one tap deep, weak story at launch.
- **B: Hero line + "Tu mes" + protection value.** **Chosen.**
- **C: B + relationship clusters + weekly batch confirmation.** Rejected for now: clustering
  needs weeks of dense BO QR data. Julian also prefers inline asks to weekly batches.

## Recommended Approach (B, with Julian's modifications)

### Scope rules (apply to every decision)
- **Account scope:** every figure is computed for the **active account from JWT context**, never
  aggregated across accounts (multi-account isolation rule). Personal and each business account
  have their own month. Business owners see their business account's month. Employees see nothing.
- **Month boundary:** calendar month in the user's timezone. The client sends its IANA timezone
  with each query. The fallback is the phone-country timezone. Travel just re-buckets on the next query. "Same day last month" =
  same day number, clamped to the last day of a shorter month (Mar 31 → Feb 28/29), up to the same
  time of day.
- **What counts as a movement:** only confirmed/completed records. Pending, failed, rejected and
  reversed records never count. A refund is booked in the month it settles, as a negative Salió. Past months are never rewritten.
- **Currency:** all hero and "Tu mes" amounts are in USD (design grammar: amounts in $). The
  protection sentence is the only local-currency (Bs) number.

### Two dimensions, never mixed
- **Kind** is always rail-derived, never user-edited. It decides which bucket a movement lands in.
- **Category** (Comida, Transporte, Casa, Familia, Trabajo, Otro) is a second, optional,
  user-supplied dimension. It applies only to `Gasto` movements in phase 1. Inbound is not categorized.
  "Sin categoría" = Gasto movements with no category. It is the only uncategorized number
  shown, and it is shown honestly.

### Kind table and bucket rules

| Source | Kind | Bucket |
|---|---|---|
| Recargar (Koywe/Infinia/Stereum deposit), crypto deposit to own wallet | Recarga | none (own money moving in, shown as a separate line) |
| Retirar to own bank / own external wallet | Retiro | none (own money moving out, separate line) |
| Inbound Confío Pay / QR / invoice payment to a business account | Venta | Entró |
| Transfer between the user's own personal ↔ business accounts | Movimiento propio | none, hidden |
| Token conversion inside the wallet (e.g. legacy cUSD → cUSD+) | Conversión | none, hidden |
| cUSD+ deposit / withdraw | Ahorro | Guardaste (net) |
| Stocks buy / sell | Inversión | Invertiste (net). Row hidden when the account has no stock movements or stocks aren't enabled |
| Payroll received | Sueldo | Entró |
| Payroll paid (business account) | Nómina | Salió |
| Inbound from a person / Confío user / external address not owned by the user | Recibido de persona | Entró |
| Rewards, invite and bonus credits | Bono | Entró (labeled "Bonos") |
| Confío Pay / business recipient | Comercio | Salió → Gasto |
| Pagar-tab QR (BO QR, Pix, AR QR) | Pago QR | Salió → Gasto |
| Enviar to a person / Confío user / external address not owned by the user | Envío a persona | Salió → Gasto |

**Ownership rule (Retiro vs Envío):** a movement is Retiro only when it was started from the
`Retirar` / "A mi propia cuenta" flow, or its destination is a saved own-account destination. Every
other outbound to an external wallet or bank is Envío a persona. When unknown, the default is Envío
(it can be moved by the edit flow). The flow, not the address, decides.

Hero line: `Entró` = sum of Entró bucket, `Salió` = sum of Salió bucket. Recargas, retiros,
savings and investments are **never** in Entró/Salió, so topping up and moving money to savings
don't distort the month. The 3-movement threshold counts only Entró + Salió movements.

### Decision 1: Home hero line
- Under the balance, inside the emerald hero and above Enviar/Recibir
  ([HomeScreen.tsx:1246](apps/src/screens/HomeScreen.tsx)):
  `Octubre · Entró $420 · Salió $310  ›`. USD only (see Scope rules).
- Neutral text only: no red/green, no deltas (calm-Home law).
- **Auto-hidden** when the month has fewer than 3 Entró + Salió movements.
- This is the only Home change. The protection sentence never appears on Home.

### Decision 2: "Tu mes" screen
- Read-only over movement data. The only writes are counterparty labels, skip counts and per-movement overrides, owned by Decision 4.
- Sections, top to bottom: Entró / Salió (vs same day last month) · protection sentence (Decision 3)
  · Gasto by category, including "Sin categoría" · Recargas and Retiros as plain lines ·
  Guardaste / Invertiste (hidden when zero) · **"Con quién"**: top 5 counterparties this month by
  combined in + out total, all kinds (people, stalls, merchants), each showing its category if set.
  No recurrence labels in phase 1, because first-month BO users have no history.
- History pattern per the design grammar: bounded preview + "Ver todos" FlatList with pagination.

### Decision 3: Protection value (the differentiator)
- Copy: **"Hoy esos dólares te costarían Bs 340 más."** Rendered only on "Tu mes", never on Home.
- **Like-for-like quote sides:** cost basis = local currency the user actually paid per USD on
  their own completed on-ramps. Today's value = Confío's **current on-ramp (buy) quote** for the
  same currency. Buy vs buy means the round-trip spread is never counted as a gain or a loss.
- Lot method (deterministic): the lot pool is the account's completed on-ramps only. Outflows
  (Salió + Retiro) deplete **non-on-ramped dollars first** (received, bonus), then on-ramp lots
  FIFO. Protected USD = the sum of remaining on-ramp lots, capped at the wallet USD balance
  (cUSD/cUSD+, excluding stocks). Cost basis = weighted average over those remaining lots only.
- Rates are **all-in effective** on both sides: local paid ÷ USD received, versus the current
  quote's local ÷ USD-delivered, from the same provider as the user's lots where possible. A
  provider or fee change can still move the number. That's accepted and noted in eng review.
- Per currency: computed only for the account's ramp currency (`useRampCountry` = phoneCountry).
  On-ramps in other currencies are excluded.
- **Show only when positive and ≥ Bs 10 (or equivalent).** Zero or negative → hide the
  sentence entirely, with no loss copy. Also hidden when no cost basis exists or no live buy quote
  exists for that currency.
- Server-side GraphQL object type (not JSONString), in its own isolated query.
- **Assumptions to validate before launch:** (a) a live BOB on-ramp quote is available at BO
  launch (Koywe BO on-ramp completed 10 orders in 90d, so likely yes; confirm the rate is
  quotable without creating an order); (b) legal/compliance review in BO and VE that showing a
  local-currency comparison derived from Confío's own quotes is acceptable. Until (b) clears, the
  sentence ships behind a flag that defaults off per country.

### Decision 4: Inline categorization ask (Julian's call: per transaction, not batched)
- Asked on the **success screen after** an outbound Gasto with no category and no counterparty
  rule ([PaymentSuccessScreen.tsx](apps/src/screens/PaymentSuccessScreen.tsx), the Bolivia QR result
  state in [BoliviaQrSendScreen.tsx:127](apps/src/screens/BoliviaQrSendScreen.tsx)).
- Optional chips. Skipping is the default. It never blocks, never gates, and nothing has to be cleared later.
- **Skip policy:** after **2 skips** for the same counterparty, stop asking for that counterparty.
  It stays labelable from "Tu mes" / transaction detail.
- **Counterparty key:** `institution_code + account_number` for local QR / bank destinations;
  Confío user id for Confío users, which wins over phone/contact when both match;
  normalized address for external wallets. Per-stall stability of BO QR account numbers is an
  **assumption to verify** against Stereum decode data in the first 2 weeks.
- **Edits:** changing a category asks "¿Solo este pago o todos los de <nombre>?". "Todos" updates the
  counterparty rule and **re-labels past and future** movements to that counterparty, except those
  with a single-payment override. "Solo este" stores a per-movement override and leaves the rule unchanged.
- Inbound is never prompted or categorized in phase 1.
- Writes go through a mutation scoped to the JWT active account: `CounterpartyLabel(account,
  counterparty_key, category, skip_count)` + per-movement override. Skip counts are server-side, so they survive device changes. Exact model is decided in eng review.

### Out of scope (phase 1)
- GPS / Google Places classification: dropped.
- Constancia / reputation proof: dropped for now. When it returns it must count only KYC'd
  counterparties and dedupe circular flows (Colombia fraud case).
- AI clustering, weekly batch confirmations, recurrence labels: revisit after 8 weeks of BO QR data.

## Open Questions
1. **Unified movement source:** reuse the existing unified history feed or build a new aggregation
   over SendTransaction, PaymentTransaction, RampTransaction, MoneyOperation (Infinia/Stereum),
   cUSD+ vault events, stocks and payroll? Dedup between a ramp record and its on-chain landing
   (e.g. "Depósito USDT recibido" sends) is the hard part. (eng review)
2. Venezuela: which rail produces a VES cost basis and a live buy quote? If none, VE launches with
   the month view but without protection value.
3. Chip set for BO vs VE: is "Transporte" the right first chip for Bolivia? Validate in the first 2 weeks.
4. Are stocks enabled for BO/VE accounts at launch? If not, the Invertiste row simply stays hidden.

## Success Criteria (first 6 weeks after BO QR launch)
- ≥30% of BO accounts with 3+ Entró/Salió movements tap the hero line at least once.
- ≥25% answer rate on the inline chips among prompts shown.
- **Classified** = a Gasto movement with a category (chip, counterparty rule or manual edit).
  Target: ≥60% of BO Gasto **volume** classified by week 6.
- Friction guardrail (no holdout, too few users to split), within-user: for each payer, compare
  the gap to their next QR payment after a chip-prompted payment vs after an unprompted one
  (answered, skipped-out, or pre-chip). A median gap increase >20% is a fail, and chips get pulled back to
  once-per-counterparty-ever.
- Instrument: events `tu_mes_opened`, `hero_cashflow_tapped`, `category_chip_answered`,
  `category_chip_skipped`, added to `CLIENT_EMITTABLE_EVENTS` in users/funnel_schema.py so they land in our DB too.

## Reviewer Concerns

Disposition: CONCERNS_RECORDED

Stop: MAX_ITERATIONS

### R3-1 — consistency

**Problem**

> The Ownership rule says an unknown outbound defaults to Envío and 'it can be moved by the edit flow', but 'Two dimensions, never mixed' says Kind is 'always rail-derived, never user-edited', and the only edit flow (Decision 4 Edits) changes a category, not a kind. A user whose withdrawal to their own wallet lands as Envío/Gasto has no specified way to fix it, or the kind rule is broken.

**Remedy**

> Choose one: allow a scoped kind correction (Envío ↔ Retiro only, as a per-movement override in the Decision 4 write model and listed in Decision 2's allowed writes), or remove 'it can be moved by the edit flow' and accept that misbucketed own-wallet withdrawals stay in Salió/Gasto.

### R3-2 — completeness

**Problem**

> The Ownership rule only covers outbound. Inbound has the same split: 'crypto deposit to own wallet' is Recarga (bucket none) while 'external address not owned by the user' is Recibido de persona (Entró), but nothing says how an incoming crypto transfer from the user's own outside wallet is told apart from one sent by a third party. Without a rule, self-funding inflates Entró or third-party income disappears as Recarga.

**Remedy**

> Define the inbound ownership signal (e.g. the Recargar/'Depositar cripto' flow, a saved own-address list, sender address matching a previous Retiro destination) and the default kind when unknown.

### R3-3 — consistency

**Problem**

> Premise 4 says 'Inbound is labeled only by manual edit in phase 1', but 'Two dimensions' says 'Inbound is not categorized' and Decision 4 says 'Inbound is never prompted or categorized in phase 1'. The premise still promises a manual inbound label that the committed behavior removes.

**Remedy**

> Update Premise 4 to say inbound movements are not categorized in phase 1.

### R3-4 — clarity

**Problem**

> The Ownership rule ends 'The flow, not the address, decides', but its own second clause makes a movement Retiro when 'its destination is a saved own-account destination', which is an address-based test. A user who saved their own bank and then pays it from Enviar gets an unclear result.

**Remedy**

> State the precedence explicitly (e.g. Retirar flow OR saved-own destination ⇒ Retiro, regardless of which flow started it) and drop or reword the 'not the address' sentence.

### R3-5 — completeness

**Problem**

> Refunds are 'booked in the month it settles, as a negative Salió', but the doc does not say how a refund of a Gasto affects 'Gasto by category' (does it inherit the original's category or land in 'Sin categoría'?), the Classified-volume metric, the 3-movement threshold, or the 'Con quién' totals. An uncategorized negative would also make 'Sin categoría' negative.

**Remedy**

> Specify that a refund inherits the original movement's kind, counterparty and category (current rule or override), reduces that category in the settling month, and state whether it counts toward the 3-movement threshold.

### R3-6 — completeness

**Problem**

> Business accounts are in scope and 'Employees see nothing', but employees can still make payments on a business account. The doc does not say whether the Decision 4 chips appear on the success screen for an employee-initiated payment, nor whether employees may call the label mutation; project rules require every mutation to validate permissions.

**Remedy**

> State that chips and the label mutation are owner-only (or gated by a named permission) for business accounts, and that the mutation validates it server-side.

### R3-7 — completeness

**Problem**

> The counterparty key covers local QR/bank destinations, Confío users and external wallets, but not Comercio payments to a Confío Pay business recipient. A business account is not a Confío user id, and keying a merchant by its owner's user id would merge the owner's personal and business accounts into one rule.

**Remedy**

> Add the key for business recipients (e.g. the business/account id) and state that it never collapses with the owner's personal user id.

### R3-8 — clarity

**Problem**

> 'Con quién' ranks 'top 5 counterparties this month by combined in + out total, all kinds (people, stalls, merchants)'. 'All kinds' could also include Recarga/Retiro providers (Koywe, Infinia), Movimiento propio and Ahorro/Inversión, which would put on-ramp providers or the user's own accounts at the top for most users.

**Remedy**

> List the included kinds explicitly (e.g. only kinds in the Entró/Salió buckets) and exclude own-money kinds.

### R3-9 — clarity

**Problem**

> The hero line is the entry point to 'Tu mes' (Approach A was rejected as hidden), yet it is auto-hidden below 3 Entró + Salió movements, while the skip policy relies on 'Tu mes' to label skipped counterparties. The doc does not say whether 'Tu mes' is reachable at all when the hero is hidden (early in the month, on day 1 of a new month, or for low-activity accounts).

**Remedy**

> State whether 'Tu mes' has a secondary entry (e.g. from the history list or transaction detail) or is intentionally unreachable below the threshold.

### R3-10 — clarity

**Problem**

> Bono ('Rewards, invite and bonus credits') is put in Entró under a rule that all amounts are USD, but the doc does not say what a bonus credit is denominated in or when it counts. If rewards are non-USD tokens or accrue off-chain until a locked claim, the USD value and the counting moment (accrual vs claim/credit to wallet) are undefined.

**Remedy**

> Define which bonus events count (e.g. only USD-denominated credits that land in the wallet) and their USD valuation, or exclude non-USD rewards from Entró.

### R3-11 — feasibility

**Problem**

> The within-user friction guardrail compares the gap after a chip-prompted payment with the gap after an unprompted one. Prompts only fire for counterparties with no rule and fewer than 2 skips, so prompted payments are mostly first or new counterparties, while unprompted ones are mostly habitual counterparties, which already have different repeat gaps. The groups differ by counterparty novelty, not only by the chip, and 'skipped-out' is undefined.

**Remedy**

> Match on counterparty novelty (e.g. compare prompted first-time-counterparty payments with pre-chip first-time-counterparty payments for the same user), and define 'skipped-out' as a counterparty that reached the 2-skip limit.
